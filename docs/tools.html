Decoder and Comparer

The small things you would otherwise paste into a website.

Decoder

Base64, URL encoding, HTML entities, hex, JWT, and MD5 and SHA-1/256/512, stackable as a chain so you can peel a value one layer at a time.

Highlight text anywhere in a message and a Decode button appears next to the selection. It opens the decoder on that text when you click it, and does nothing at all if you were only selecting something to copy.

Comparer

Two requests or two responses, side by side, with a word or byte diff. The usual job: the same request as two different users, where the interesting part is the three bytes that differ.
